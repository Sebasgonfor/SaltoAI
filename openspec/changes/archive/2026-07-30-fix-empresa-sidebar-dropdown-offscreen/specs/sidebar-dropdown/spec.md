## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Empresa sidebar account dropdown opens within viewport
The `UserButton` at the bottom of the `EmpresaSidebar` SHALL render its dropdown menu opening upward (`top-left` placement) so that all menu items remain visible and clickable within the browser viewport.

#### Scenario: Empresa user opens account menu from sidebar
- **WHEN** an empresa user clicks the `UserButton` at the bottom of the sidebar
- **THEN** the dropdown menu opens above the button, fully within the visible viewport
